# ThinkEx Workspace

ThinkEx workspaces hold the durable study materials a student creates, imports, records, and studies with AI.

## Language

**Recording**:
A first-class workspace item containing retained lecture audio and its time-aligned transcript.
_Avoid_: Recording session, transcript document

**Segment**:
A contiguous, independently playable portion of a Recording.
_Avoid_: Multipart part, blob chunk

**Transcript cue**:
A time-aligned passage in a Recording that links the transcript to its audio position.
_Avoid_: Caption chunk, page
